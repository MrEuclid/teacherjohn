Q1 - Sort the fractions from smallest to biggest

Putting fractions in order

The easiest way is to change the fractions to percentages.
Example
Is $ \frac{2}{11} < \frac{3}{13} $ ?
$ \frac{2}{11} \times 100\% = \frac{200}{11} \% $ = 18.18%
$ \frac{3}{13} \times 100\% = \frac{300}{13} \% $ = 23.08%
Therefore $ \frac{2}{11} $ is less than $ \frac{3}{13} $ .
